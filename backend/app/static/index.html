Financial Credential Theft
CRITICAL

Threatens immediate account freeze or debit card block. Scammers direct victims to malicious phishing forms or APK packages that hijack SMS OTPs.

"Dear SBI Customer, your YONO account is blocked today due to pending PAN KYC. Update at http://192.168.1.10/sbi-verify.apk"
🛡️ Defense: Banks never distribute APK files or send KYC links via 10-digit mobile numbers.

Electricity / Power Cutoff Threat

HIGH RISK

"Dear consumer, your electricity connection will be cut off tonight at 9:30 PM. Immediately contact officer 9876543210."
🛡️ Defense: Electricity discoms provide 15-day statutory physical notice, never disconnect power at night.

Layer 2: Calibrated Logistic Regression (2.2ms)

Decomposed sparse weights (weights.npz) loading in 19ms with 2.5MB RAM and 0.9959 ROC-AUC.

Layer 3: Groq LLM Second Opinion (~0.7s)

Triggered only on borderline cases (0.35-0.65). SHA-256 hash caching, 5s timeout, 50 requests/day per user.
